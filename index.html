Eevee

Eevee has an unstable genetic makeup that suddenly mutates due to the environment in which it lives. Radiation from various stones causes this Pokémon to evolve.

Vaporeon Evolve Eevee with a water stone
Jolteon Evolve Eevee with a thunder stone
Flareon Evolve Eevee with a fire stone
Espeon Evolve Eevee with high friendship during the day(fan games use the sun stone)
Umbreon Evolve Eevee with high friendship during the night(fan games use the moon stone)
Leafeon Evolve Eevee near a mossy rock(new games use the leaf stone)
Glaceon Evolve Eevee near an icy rock(new games use the ice stone)
Sylveon Evolve Eevee with high affection while knowing a fairy-type move(fan games use the shiny stone)
